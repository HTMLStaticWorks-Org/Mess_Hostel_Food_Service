Uncompromised Kitchen Standards

A Mess Kitchen You Can Walk Into Any Time

Most hostel messes hide their kitchens behind closed doors. At Aangan Rasoi, students and parents are invited to walk through our prep, storage, and washing zones any day between 10:00 AM and 8:00 PM.

RO + UV Drinking & Cooking Water

Every drop used in kneading dough, boiling dal, and table jugs passes multi-stage filtration.

Cold-Pressed Mustard & Sunflower Oil

Strictly zero palm oil, zero vanaspati, and never re-heating leftover frying oil.

3-Sink Hot Water Steel Sanitization

Thalis, katoris, and spoons are washed with food-grade detergent and rinsed at 75°C.

Strictly Separate Veg & Egg/Meat Zones

Dedicated utensils, color-coded chopping boards, and separate serving counters.
