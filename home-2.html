What Goes Into Your Thali

Home-Grade Groceries, Never Bulk Commercial Shortcuts

You can taste the difference between commercial hostel food and homely cooking in the raw ingredients we stock in our pantry.

Inspect Ingredient Sourcing

100% Chakki Atta

Zero maida mixed in our rotis or parathas. Our phulkas stay soft until evening because we knead fresh dough with warm filtered water.

Unpolished Dals

We soak unpolished toor, moong, chana, and rajma for 4+ hours before cooking so they are gentle on digestion and never need baking soda.

Masala Blends

Whole coriander, cumin, black pepper, and Kashmiri chillies are sun-dried and ground in small batches for authentic aroma without throat burn.

Farm Vegetable

Seasonal leafy greens, gourds, cauliflower, and tomatoes arrive every morning and are triple-washed in ozonated & salted RO water.
