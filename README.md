# Aangan Rasoi — Mess & Hostel Food Service HTML Template

A complete, responsive, ThemeForest & Creative Market grade HTML5/CSS3/JS template designed for student mess, hostel dining, and homestyle tiffin services.

## Pages Included
- [`index.html`](index.html) — **Home 1 (Classic Homely Hospitality)**: Hero thali showcase, interactive Today's Menu preview, weekly highlights, interactive meal plan pricing cards, open-kitchen hygiene summary, student benefits, and testimonials.
- [`home-2.html`](home-2.html) — **Home 2 (Editorial Dining & Menu Showcase)**: Asymmetric editorial bento hero, ingredient provenance cards, full 7-day rotating menu matrix table, 4-column meal pass snapshot, and dining hall story.
- [`about.html`](about.html) — **About Us**: Brand origin story, homestyle culinary philosophy, 5:30 AM to 9:30 PM kitchen preparation rhythm, and student-first commitments.
- [`meal-plans.html`](meal-plans.html) — **Meal Plans**: 6 comprehensive meal plans with interactive Monthly (30 Days) vs. Weekly Trial (7 Days) and Pure Veg vs. Veg + Egg/Non-Veg toggles, side-by-side comparison table, serving schedule, and FAQs.
- [`hygiene.html`](hygiene.html) — **Hygiene Standards**: Open-door kitchen walk-in policy, 6 pillars of daily hygiene, 4-shift daily sanitation schedule, and realistic food handling commitments.
- [`contact.html`](contact.html) — **Contact & Meal Enquiry**: Reception phone, WhatsApp desk, email, social community channels, validated Student Meal Enquiry form with URL query plan pre-selection, and campus neighborhood map with directions link.
- [`dashboard.html`](dashboard.html) — **Student Dashboard**: Comprehensive portal for viewing weekly rotating menus, managing meal plans (switch plan, request pause days), and paying fees (UPI, Netbanking, Cards) with simulated receipt download. Features a full-height left sidebar with user profile, navigation items, and bottom logout action.
- [`login.html`](login.html) — **Student Login UI**: Clean single-card authentication layout without navbar or footer; brandmark in top center, single-column input fields, password toggle, OTP reset drawer, and Google & Apple social sign-in buttons at bottom.
- [`signup.html`](signup.html) — **Student Sign Up UI**: Registration card with brandmark in top center, all input fields on separate lines, preferred meal plan, dietary preference pills, rules agreement, and Google & Apple social sign-up buttons.

## Key Features & Customizations
- **Brand Favicon**: Custom vector SVG cloche favicon matching the exact navbar brand mark.
- **RTL Language Toggle**: One-click "RTL" navbar button styled identically to the theme toggle, enabling full bidirectional layout support (`dir="rtl"`) persisted in `localStorage`.
- **Light/Dark Mode with White Footer**: Full theme switcher with readable contrast. In light mode, the footer background is clean white (`#FFFFFF`) featuring interactive social media buttons (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, X/Twitter) with smooth hover effects.
- **Consistent Page Heroes**: All non-home pages (`about.html`, `meal-plans.html`, `hygiene.html`, `contact.html`) share a uniform hero height (`280px`).
- **Authentic Hostel Food Photography**: 7 custom, relatable food photographs showcasing authentic steel dining thalis, mess dining halls, fresh iron-tawa rotis, south Indian breakfasts, hostel samosas, and commercial kitchen vegetable prep.
- **Tablet & 1024px Responsive Grids**: Sections automatically format into 2 cards per row on tablets and 1024px viewports, with any last odd-number card centered and featured cards spanning the full row.
- **Card Tag Centering**: All dietary badges, timing pills, and ribbons inside cards are centered.
- **Separate-Line Card Typography**: All card titles and descriptive subtext are structured on distinct separate lines.
- **Zero Dependencies**: 100% pure semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 variables (`css/styles.css`), and vanilla JavaScript (`js/main.js`).
