Our Story & Culinary Purpose

Started With One Simple Question: Why Can't Hostel Food Feel Like Home?

Aangan Rasoi was born in 2019 when two former engineering hostelers teamed up with veteran family cooks in Vidya Nagar to replace watery dals and oily tiffins with honest, comforting daily meals.

Our Food Philosophy

Restaurant Food Is Built for One Night. Homely Food Is Built for Every Day.

When you eat out at a restaurant once a week, heavy cream, excess oil, and fiery red chilli paste taste exciting. When you eat that same food twice a day as a student, it drains your energy and upsets your stomach.

At Aangan Rasoi, we cook the way Indian households cook: using tempered whole spices, slow-soaked unpolished dals, fresh ginger-garlic-coriander bases, and 100% whole-wheat Sharbati atta. Our goal is that after finishing a full lunch thali, you feel nourished and alert for your 2:00 PM lecture—never sluggish.

Behind the Kitchen Doors

How We Prepare 4 Fresh Meals Every Single Day

We never pre-cook gravies for the week. Every meal follows a disciplined daily rhythm in our kitchen.

05:30 AM – 07:15 AM

Dawn Vegetable Intake & Breakfast Prep

Fresh vegetables, milk, and curd arrive at 5:30 AM. While vegetables are triple-washed, our morning team steams idlis, tempers poha, and brews fresh ginger-cardamom chai in brass vessels.

09:45 AM – 12:15 PM

Slow-Simmered Lunch & Fresh Dough

Unpolished dals and rajma/chole soak for 4 hours before pressure cooking. Whole-wheat dough is kneaded with warm RO water at 11:15 AM so phulkas puff soft and hot on the iron tawa from 12:30 PM.

04:00 PM – 05:00 PM

Evening Snack & Kulhad Tea Service

After the afternoon kitchen reset, our snack station prepares fresh beetroot cutlets, sweet corn chaat, or grilled chutney sandwiches alongside steaming kulhad tea for students returning from labs.

06:00 PM – 09:30 PM

Light, Comforting Evening Dinner

Evening meals are intentionally seasoned lighter for restful sleep and late-night study sessions—featuring lasooni dal tadka, seasonal sabzis, paneer/egg curries, and warm homemade kheer or halwa twice a week.

Student-First Hospitality

Our 4 Commitments to Every Student Living Away From Home

We know student life comes with exam stress, tight monthly allowances, and occasional homesickness. Our mess rules are designed with empathy, not rigid bureaucracy:

Sick-Day Care Without Extra Charge

If you have a fever or stomach upset, message our desk for soft Ghee Moong Dal Khichdi, curd, and warm turmeric milk packed in a clean steel carrier.

Fair Pause & Carry-Forward Policy

Going home for Diwali, semester break, or a family function? Pause your pass with 12 hours' notice and carry forward your unused days.

Monthly Student Menu Council

Our menu isn't dictated from above. Every month, student representatives review dishes, drop unpopular sabzis, and vote on regional weekend specials.
