Meal Timings & Serving Schedule

We align our serving windows with university morning lectures, afternoon lab breaks, and evening coaching classes.

Morning Breakfast: 07:30 AM – 09:30 AM

Dietary Clarity

How Our Pure Veg & Non-Veg Options Work

We respect the dietary traditions of every student household. Our kitchen and dining hall follow strict separation protocols:

100% Pure Vegetarian Line

Cooked in the main vegetarian kitchen with green-coded utensils. Includes Paneer specials 3 times a week, Soya/Mushroom/Kofta specials, and Jain (no onion-garlic) dal/sabzi available on prior request.

Veg + Egg / Homestyle Chicken Line

Prepared in an isolated kitchen bay using red-coded cookware and served at a dedicated counter. Includes Boiled Eggs/Omelette at breakfast, Egg Curry on Mondays & Thursdays, and Homestyle Chicken Curry/Biryani on Wednesdays, Fridays & Sundays.
